Hyderabad, Sindh — after sunset
A cinematic 3D portrait, built in Blender, filmed in the browser.

Eleven towns: Saddar, Latifabad, Qasimabad, Hirabad, GOR Colony, Auto Bhan,
S.I.T.E., Paretabad, Phuleli, Bhitai Nagar, Tando Yousaf.

Not a game — a camera that drives itself between the districts while each card
names the town in English and Urdu.

Facades with balconies, AC units and grime. A film grade in the renderer.
2.9 MB glTF (Draco) · 147,732 faces · one sunset.

Live: https://ahmedalighori.github.io/hyderabad-city/
