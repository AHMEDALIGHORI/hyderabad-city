# Brag plan — Hyderabad, Sindh: after sunset

## What the project is
A cinematic 3D portrait of Hyderabad, Sindh: eleven real towns (Saddar, Latifabad,
Qasimabad, Hirabad, GOR Colony, Auto Bhan, S.I.T.E., Paretabad, Phuleli, Bhitai Nagar,
Tando Yousaf) generated procedurally in Blender, loaded with Draco (19.4 MB → 817 KB)
and filmed in the browser with Three.js. Not a game: no controls, a camera that
drives itself between districts while cards name each town in English and Urdu.

## Angle
"It is not a game" — the video answers a misreading before it happens. The city is
shot like a place, not a level: wide aerials, street-level stills, and a card language
that reads like a travel film.

## Rubric answers
- Hook: "Hyderabad, after sunset." over the widest aerial.
- Product proof: real stills from the build — aerials, shopfronts, a rickshaw in the
  headlights, a night street.
- Specificity: real town names, Urdu script, the actual numbers (147,732 faces, 817 KB).
- CTA: the live URL.
